## 🎯 The picture at Day 6

### What's working (US)
- **CPL = $4.80** — among the best I've seen for SaaS messaging campaigns
- **Daily convs accelerating**: 2 → 0 → 8 → 2 → 5 → 7 → 3 (the 8 on Day 3 and 7 on Day 6 show Meta has found the audience)
- **Men 25-54 = 85% of conversions** — clear targeting signal
- **FB Feed = 9 convs at $3.86 ea** — the workhorse placement
- **Depth-5 messages on 3 conversations** — these are people actively engaging back-and-forth, real qualified leads

### What's broken (UK)
- **188 clicks → 0 chats over 7 days = systemic break**, not a small-sample fluke
- 137 page engagements, 41 reactions, 37 likes, 3 comments — people LIKE the ad, they just don't message
- Most likely root cause: the **WhatsApp deep-link experience**. When a UK user taps the CTA:
  - The ad creative says PixieBytes, but the WhatsApp chat opens with **+31 (Netherlands)** as the recipient
  - UK tradies see a foreign number — trust collapses, they back out
  - This is exactly why the click→chat conversion is 0% in UK and 35% in US (where users probably also see +31 but it's all foreign to them anyway, so doesn't trigger the trust gap)

### Targeting cohort — validated
**Men 25-54 is the buyer.** Across both campaigns:
- 23/27 US conversations from male 25-54
- 121/188 UK clicks from male 25-54 (still 0 chats but they're the ones tapping)
