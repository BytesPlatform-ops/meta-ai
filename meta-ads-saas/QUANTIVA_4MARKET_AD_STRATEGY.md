## 0. Executive summary — read this first

Quantiva sits at the intersection of **three** Meta-restricted categories simultaneously:
1. **Financial products & services** → mandatory `FINANCIAL_PRODUCTS_SERVICES` Special Ad Category (SAC)
2. **Cryptocurrency exchange/trading** → mandatory Meta Crypto Ad Authorization (Verified Crypto Advertiser badge)
3. **Investment advice** (signals, VC Pools) → may trigger jurisdiction-specific securities licensing

This means **before any of these 4 markets will accept a single dollar of Quantiva spend**, you must complete:

| Step | Owner | Time | Blocker if skipped |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business verification in Meta Business Manager | You | 2-7 days | All FPS ads rejected |
| Identity verification (March 2026 mandate) | You | 1-3 days | All financial ads rejected |
| Crypto Advertiser Authorization (per market) | You | 7-30 days | All crypto creative rejected |
| Local regulator license OR licensed partner per market | You / partner | 4-12 weeks | Country-specific rejection |
| SAC selection on every campaign | Code (already done) | — | Account-level restriction |
| Mandatory risk disclaimer in every creative | Copy | — | Creative rejection |

**If you don't yet hold local licenses in all 4 markets**, the practical path is:
- UAE + AU: **most feasible** — clear license pathways, mature crypto-ad approval flows
- ZA: feasible but slower (FSCA CASP licensing pipeline ~12 months)
- NZ: hardest — small market, narrow FMA pathway, low Meta volume

Budget recommendation if you launch all 4 today: **start at 50% AU + 30% UAE + 15% ZA + 5% NZ**. Why is in §7.

## 1. The regulatory layer cake

Quantiva's product description mentions: Binance, Bybit, Alpaca, AI signals, auto-execution, sentiment, VC Pools (pooled investment), KYC via Sumsub. Each of these triggers a different regulator:

| Quantiva feature | Triggers regulator under |
|---|---|
| Crypto signals / auto-trade | Crypto-asset trading rules (UAE-VARA, AU-AFSL, ZA-FSCA CASP, NZ-FMA) |
| Stock signals / Alpaca | Securities laws (DFSA/ADGM, ASIC, FSCA FSP, FMA FSP) |
| VC Pools (other people's capital) | **Fund management / collective investment scheme** licensing — strictest tier |
| AI sentiment + market commentary | Financial advisory / general advice rules |
| Paper trading mode | Generally exempt — no real-money risk |
| KYC + custody | AML / counter-terrorism financing rules |

Meta's authorization process **cross-checks against local regulator databases**. If Quantiva isn't registered in (or partnered with someone registered in) a target market, the campaign gets rejected at review.

## 2. Per-market deep dive

### 🇦🇪 UAE

**Audience snapshot**
- 9.4M Facebook users; UAE has the world's highest social media penetration (~100% per Kepios)
- Average user spends **31h 8min/month on Facebook** — highest globally
- English + Arabic both work; English is dominant in Dubai/Abu Dhabi for fintech audiences
- High disposable income; strong crypto adoption (~25% own crypto per various surveys)

**Regulatory pathway — three valid licenses Meta accepts:**
1. **VARA** (Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority — Dubai) — primary path for crypto
2. **ADGM FSRA** (Abu Dhabi Global Market) — for crypto + stocks
3. **DFSA** (Dubai Financial Services Authority) — for investment tokens
4. **Central Bank of UAE** — for stored-value facilities

**Hard rules to know:**
- VARA Marketing Regulations (issued late 2025) apply to **any** business marketing virtual assets in UAE — even foreign companies must comply
- Penalty for unlicensed crypto marketing: up to **AED 10M** (~$2.7M USD)
- Must be VARA-licensed OR act on behalf of a VARA-licensed VASP
- Mandatory risk warning text in every crypto ad

**Cultural / creative notes:**
- "Halal-compliant trading" angle resonates — Islamic finance principles around riba (interest)
- Avoid "guaranteed returns" language — VARA strictly enforces
- Government push for cashless transactions (Dubai targets 90% cashless by end-2026) creates tailwind
- Local trust signals matter: "DIFC-based", "regulated by VARA" carry weight

### 🇦🇺 Australia

**Audience snapshot**
- ~17M Facebook users (~65% of population)
- ~23% of Australians have owned crypto (Independent Reserve State of Crypto 2025)
- English; very price/comparison-oriented audience

**Regulatory pathway:**
- **AUSTRAC** Digital Currency Exchange registration (still required)
- **AFSL** (Australian Financial Services Licence) from ASIC — **NEW mandatory** under Corporations Amendment (Digital Assets Framework) Act 2026
- AFSL application deadline: **30 June 2026** — platforms must lodge by then or face ASIC enforcement
- Travel Rule compliance from **31 March 2026**

**Hard rules:**
- Exemption for *very small* platforms (under $10M annual flow AND under $5K per customer) — Quantiva likely doesn't qualify
- Stock features need separate AFSL with relevant authorizations (advice vs. dealing)
- Meta-side: must hold AFSL OR AUSTRAC + clear authorization in Business Manager

**Cultural / creative notes:**
- Strong DIY / "self-managed super fund" investor culture
- ASIC actively flags scam ads; "AI trading" + "guaranteed" = instant takedown
- Disclaimer-heavy creative is the norm and trusted; Australians read fine print
- Tradie + small business owner demographic is huge crypto buyer cohort

### 🇿🇦 South Africa

**Audience snapshot**
- Southern Africa region: ~29.8M Facebook users
- South Africa itself: ~26M FB users
- Africa's largest crypto market by volume (Chainalysis)
- Mostly English with Afrikaans, Zulu, Xhosa secondary

**Regulatory pathway:**
- **FSCA CASP license** (Crypto Asset Service Provider) — operational since June 2023
- Required subcategory: **FSP Subcategory 1.28 (Crypto Assets)**
- As of 2026: 300+ CASP licenses issued, FSCA has matured the process
- Stock features need standard **FSP license** under FAIS Act

**Hard rules:**
- FSCA classifies crypto as a "financial product" since Oct 2022 — full FAIS Act applies
- Advisory category: if you give signals → "advice"; if you auto-execute → "intermediary services" — both require separate FSP authorization
- Meta accepts FSCA CASP as the crypto-ad authorization document

**Cultural / creative notes:**
- High mobile penetration, low desktop usage — mobile-first creative essential
- Strong "yield chasing" culture — high-yield messaging works but is also scam-heavy, so trust signals critical
- Local exchange brands (VALR, Luno) are reference points; positioning against them works
- Currency volatility (ZAR weakness) drives crypto interest

### 🇳🇿 New Zealand

**Audience snapshot**
- ~3.8M Facebook users (small market)
- ~6-10% crypto ownership
- English; FMA-savvy, conservative investor culture

**Regulatory pathway:**
- **FMA** (Financial Markets Authority) regulates crypto only when it qualifies as a "financial product" under the Financial Markets Conduct Act
- Most signal/auto-trade services DO qualify as financial products
- Must register as a **Financial Service Provider (FSP)** under the FSP Act
- New **Financial Advice Code** applies if Quantiva gives signals = "advice"
- Stock features: same FSP framework

**Hard rules:**
- FMA is **actively warning about AI-trading scam ads on Meta** (May 2026: 110 scam ads found in 24h on Meta surfaces)
- New 1:1 redemption + reserve audit standards being drafted late 2026 for stablecoin issuers
- Meta does not list NZ in its public crypto eligibility list — likely requires direct engagement with Meta's regional policy team
- Cheapest CPMs of the 4 markets but smallest audience

**Cultural / creative notes:**
- Conservative tone, sceptical of bold claims — "boring & reliable" beats "moonshot"
- "Backed by AI from Quantiva" beats "Make $X overnight"
- Heavy emphasis on consumer protection messaging will lift CTR
- Note: NZ users are highly aware of FMA scam warnings — credibility signals matter more than in any other market

## 3. Special Ad Category (SAC) targeting — what survives, what doesn't

Once `special_ad_categories: ["FINANCIAL_PRODUCTS_SERVICES"]` is set on the campaign (your system already does this via `sac_targeting_filter.py`), Meta applies these restrictions globally — same in all 4 markets:

### ❌ Auto-stripped at campaign create (your `sac_recovery.py` will detect these as `heavy`/`total_strip`)
- Age targeting narrower than 18-65+ — forced to full range
- Gender targeting — forced to "All"
- ZIP/postcode precision — forced to 15-mile minimum radius
- All Lookalike Audiences (LAL)
- All Income / Net Worth / Financial Behavior segments
- Specific exchange brands as interests: **Binance, Bybit, Kraken, Coinbase, Robinhood, eToro**
- Specific broker brands: most retail broker brands strip
- "Day trading", "Forex", "Margin trading" — often stripped
- Custom Audiences from CRM data (some types — varies)
- Detailed targeting expansion may be forced ON

### ✅ Survives SAC FPS — your safe interest pool

These tend to survive across all 4 markets. Test 3-5 per adset; let Advantage+ audience expansion find the rest:

**Tier 1 — broad concept interests (highest survival rate):**
- Investment (concept)
- Stock market
- Personal finance
- Financial independence
- Financial planning
- Wealth management
- Economics
- Business

**Tier 2 — publication interests (very high survival):**
- Bloomberg L.P.
- The Wall Street Journal
- Financial Times
- Forbes
- The Economist
- Business Insider
- CoinDesk (crypto-flavored, may strip in non-crypto-eligible markets)
- CNBC

**Tier 3 — broader behavior approximations:**
- Entrepreneurship
- Small business
- Technology (broad)
- Fintech (concept)
- Mobile banking
- Online banking

**Tier 4 — country-specific publications (use ONE per market):**
- 🇦🇪 UAE: Khaleej Times, Gulf News, The National
- 🇦🇺 AU: Australian Financial Review, ABC News, news.com.au
- 🇿🇦 ZA: Moneyweb, Business Day, Fin24
- 🇳🇿 NZ: NZ Herald, Stuff (business), Newshub

### ✅ Replacement for Lookalikes — Special Ad Audiences (SAA)

LAL is dead under SAC FPS. Replace with:
1. **Special Ad Audience** — Meta's SAC-compliant lookalike. Built from a Custom Audience but uses only behavior signals (no demographic) — typically ~30% less precise than LAL but legal
2. **Custom Audiences from your data** — website visitors, app installs, engagement audiences usually survive (test per market)
3. **Advantage+ audience expansion** — turn ON (your system already does for SAC), Meta finds adjacent users algorithmically

## 4. Objective recommendation per stage

### Stage 1 — "Awareness / fill the pixel" (week 1-2)
- **Objective:** `OUTCOME_TRAFFIC`
- **Optimization:** `LINK_CLICKS`
- **Why:** No pixel data yet → can't optimize for Leads usefully. Drive cheap clicks to `quantivahq.com/onboarding/sign-up` to build pixel signal first
- **Daily budget:** $20-50 per market (UAE/AU); $10-20 (ZA/NZ)

### Stage 2 — "Lead generation" (week 3-6, once you have 50+ pixel sign-ups)
- **Objective:** `OUTCOME_LEADS`
- **Optimization:** `OFFSITE_CONVERSIONS` → `COMPLETE_REGISTRATION` event
- **Why:** Now Meta has signal — let it hunt for sign-up-likely users
- **Daily budget:** $50-100 UAE/AU; $20-40 ZA/NZ

### Stage 3 — "Pro/Elite plan conversion" (week 6+)
- **Objective:** `OUTCOME_SALES` (if you have Purchase event firing from Stripe)
- **Optimization:** `OFFSITE_CONVERSIONS` → `PURCHASE` event
- **Why:** Now optimizing for revenue, not just sign-ups
- **Daily budget:** retarget-only initially — small ($10-20) but high-LTV

### Why NOT Engagement objective
Quantiva is not a messaging-funnel product (like Pixie). No WhatsApp click-to-chat flow. Skip ENGAGEMENT entirely.

### Why NOT Instant Forms (yet)
Quantiva's onboarding is multi-step (sign up → KYC → connect exchange) — can't compress into a Meta Instant Form without confusing the user. Maybe useful later for waitlist sign-ups for VC Pools specifically (single field: email).

## 5. Creative compliance — what every Quantiva ad MUST contain

Across all 4 markets, your `creative compliance gate` should enforce these in body copy (SAC Phase 5 in your roadmap — recommend building it for Quantiva):

### Required elements
| Element | Why | Example phrasing |
|---|---|---|
| Risk warning | UAE-VARA, ASIC, FSCA, FMA all require it | *"Trading involves the risk of capital loss. Past performance is not indicative of future results."* |
| License/regulator reference | Meta authorization gate + local rule | *"Quantiva is licensed by [VARA / ASIC AFSL / FSCA / FMA]"* |
| Target age | Already enforced by SAC | (system level — 18+) |
| No "guaranteed", "risk-free", "easy money" | Universal rule | Never use these terms |
| No specific return numbers | Universal rule | NO: "Make 80% returns"; OK: "AI-scored signals" |
| Clear identification of advertiser | EU AI Act-style transparency | "By Quantiva HQ" / Page identity |

### Forbidden creative patterns
- ❌ Testimonials with specific dollar returns
- ❌ Before/after wealth screenshots
- ❌ "Beat the market" claims
- ❌ Implied government endorsement
- ❌ Misleading celebrity associations (FMA flagged this specifically in May 2026)
- ❌ "Trade like the pros" — implies professional outcomes
- ❌ Pseudo-urgency ("limited spots", "deadline today") — counts as deceptive under FCA-style rules
- ❌ Specific token names in body ("Buy BTC now") — triggers manual review

### Allowed angles that work for Quantiva
- ✅ "AI-powered analysis you'd otherwise need a quant team for"
- ✅ "Paper-trade first — zero risk, real markets"
- ✅ "One dashboard for your stocks and crypto"
- ✅ "Stop juggling tabs — sentiment + signals + execution in one place"
- ✅ Educational angles: "Learn how AI scores trading sentiment"
- ✅ Comparison vs. doing it manually (time-saving)

## 6. Per-market creative direction (what works locally)

### UAE
- **Lead angle:** "Built for the Dubai investor who values their time"
- **Visual:** modern Arabic-English bilingual UI screenshots, Dubai skyline
- **Trust signal:** "VARA-licensed" badge if applicable, AED currency in screenshots
- **Avoid:** anything implying gambling (`gharar`/`maysir` — both haram)

### Australia
- **Lead angle:** "AI signals + auto-execution — built for self-directed Aussie investors"
- **Visual:** clean dashboard, AUD figures, ASX + crypto side-by-side
- **Trust signal:** "AFSL licensed" badge, ASIC mention
- **Avoid:** anything that smells like "get rich quick" — ASIC penalty risk

### South Africa
- **Lead angle:** "Stop missing crypto moves — AI watches the market for you, 24/7"
- **Visual:** mobile screenshots prominent (mobile-first audience), ZAR figures
- **Trust signal:** "FSCA CASP-licensed", credible local reference (e.g., "Used by traders in Cape Town and Johannesburg")
- **Avoid:** Western "rich influencer" imagery — doesn't convert in ZA; use realistic local imagery

### New Zealand
- **Lead angle:** "Smart trading for Kiwis who want to invest like a fund — not gamble"
- **Visual:** conservative, professional, NZD figures
- **Trust signal:** "FSP-registered" + clear FMA disclaimer
- **Avoid:** any urgency, any FOMO — Kiwis sceptical of both

## 8. Audience replacement strategy (Custom Audiences > Interests)

Under SAC, interests are weak. Real signal lives in your own data. Build these *now* so your campaigns can use them within 30 days:

### Custom Audiences you should have ready
1. **Website visitors (last 30 days)** — Meta Pixel on quantivahq.com → auto-builds
2. **Sign-up started but didn't complete** — pixel `Lead` event filtered to "no Purchase" — retargeting gold
3. **Email list from waitlist** — hash-uploaded
4. **Engagement audience** — anyone who interacted with Quantiva FB/IG content in last 90d
5. **Video viewers** — anyone watched 50%+ of a Quantiva educational video (build this with the awareness-stage ads)

### Special Ad Audiences (LAL replacement)
Once you have a **seed audience of 1,000+** (sign-ups, customers, or even engaged video viewers), create:
- 1% SAA from sign-up audience → prospecting
- 1-5% SAA from paying customers → quality prospecting
- 1% SAA from engagement audience → cold cheap audience
