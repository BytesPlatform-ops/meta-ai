## Recommendations

### 1. Install a Meta Pixel ⚠️ (High Priority)
Neither campaign has conversion tracking. Without a pixel, Meta cannot optimize for meaningful actions (form fills, sign-ups, page engagement). This is the single biggest improvement to make.

### 2. Traffic Campaign — Optimize Placements
- **Keep:** Facebook Search ($0.23/click) and Facebook Feed ($0.62/click)
- **Remove:** Instagram Feed — it burned $64.75 for only 10 clicks. Removing this placement alone would have saved ~30% of the budget or delivered ~100 more clicks

### 3. Traffic Campaign — Narrow Age Targeting
- Ages 18-34 cost $3+ per click vs $0.48 for 65+
- If the target audience is genuinely all ages, consider separate ad sets with different creatives for younger vs older audiences
- If age doesn't matter, let Meta optimize but monitor regularly

### 4. Awareness Campaign — Restrict Placements
- 96% of delivery going to Marketplace is suboptimal for awareness
- Restrict to Facebook Feed + Instagram Feed/Reels for more impactful awareness
- Marketplace impressions are cheap but low-attention

### 5. Test Different Creatives
- Both campaigns used the same video — there's no creative comparison data
- Test at least 2-3 different creatives (different hooks, formats, messaging angles)
- This is essential to find what actually resonates vs. running a single untested creative

### 6. Consider Campaign Objective
- If the goal is website visits → Traffic campaign is working well, scale it
- If the goal is just brand visibility → Awareness is fine but fix the placement issue
- If the goal is leads/sign-ups → Switch to a Leads objective with a form
